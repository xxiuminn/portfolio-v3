Like most graduates, I walked into my first marketing job curious about everything and clueless about most of it.

That phase did’t last long. Once I got a hang of the fundamentals, something started bothering me. Budgets were being allocated across channels based on gut feel. Google was consistently getting credits over social channels. I followed what my manager did, assumed it was normal, and carried on, but it never sat right. Performance marketing was supposed to be measurable, so why were the decisions so… instinctive?

That frustration pushed me to explore a career outside marketing entirely. I was even offered a software engineering role and genuinely considered it. TLDR I turned it down (oops sorry to disappoint), because I realised the bigger opportunity was bringing the engineering mindset into marketing. If decisions are made illogically, I should be the one fixing it.

So here’s a part of me during my quater-life crisis, building apps from scratch before Claude's era.

## Chores Scheduler

An app that simplifies scheduling and managing household tasks among family members.

Assign tasks, set schedules, and hold people accountable, all in one place. I even wired up Stripe webhooks for it, which at the time felt like witchcraft.

**Tech stack:** React.js, Node.js, Express.js, TanStack, PostgreSQL, Stripe Webhook, AWS EC2

[GitHub](https://github.com/xxiuminn/chores-scheduler)

## Doodle Jump

A Doodle Jump inspired platformer game.

My first ever coding project using nothing but vanilla JavaScript, HTML, and CSS.

Getting the physics to feel right was unexpectedly hard. But watching the little character bounce up the screen for the first time made it worth it.

**Tech stack:** JavaScript, HTML, CSS

[GitHub](https://github.com/xxiuminn/doodle-jump-game) · [Live Site](https://xxiuminn.github.io/doodle-jump-game/)

## Service Marketplace

A marketplace app that connects homeowners with vendors offering home services.

Think of it as a Carousell but for home services. Homeowners can browse and book vendors. Vendors can list their services and manage requests.

Building the two-sided marketplace logic, making sure both user types had the right views and the right permissions.

**Tech stack:** React.js, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, Mongoose

[GitHub](https://github.com/Serenula/Skillful-Hands)

## Flights Search Engine

A flight search engine that hunts down the best prices from various airlines. Save and snag the cheapest deal for your next trip.

Pull in prices from multiple sources, surface the best deals, and let users save what they find before it disappears.

**Tech stack:** React.js, TanStack, REST APIs

[GitHub](https://github.com/xxiuminn/flights-search-engine)
