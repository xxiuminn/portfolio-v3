Here is a fun fact about me: even though I am a marketer by profession, I was once offered a software engineering job.

Before generative AI became everyone's coding assistant, I used to spend my free time building full stack web applications from scratch. No Copilot. No ChatGPT. Just documentation, Stack Overflow, and a lot of error messages I had to figure out myself.

I still build things now, just with AI in the mix. But these projects below are from the era before that, when every bug fix felt like a small personal victory.

Here are some of the builds.

---

## Chores Scheduler

An app that simplifies scheduling and managing household tasks among family members.

Anyone who has lived with other people knows the pain. Someone always forgets to do the dishes. The chore roster falls apart by week two. Nobody can agree on who is responsible for what.

I built this to solve exactly that. Assign tasks, set schedules, and actually hold people accountable, all in one place. I even wired up Stripe webhooks for it, which at the time felt like witchcraft.

**Tech stack:** React.js, Node.js, Express.js, TanStack, PostgreSQL, Stripe Webhook, AWS EC2

[GitHub](https://github.com/xxiuminn/chores-scheduler)

## Doodle Jump

A Doodle Jump inspired platformer game.

This one was pure fun. This was my first ever coding project using nothing but vanilla JavaScript, HTML, and CSS.

Getting the physics to feel right was unexpectedly hard. But watching the little character bounce up the screen for the first time made it worth it.

**Tech stack:** JavaScript, HTML, CSS

[GitHub](https://github.com/xxiuminn/doodle-jump-game) · [Live Site](https://xxiuminn.github.io/doodle-jump-game/)

## Service Marketplace

A marketplace app that connects homeowners with vendors offering home services, all in one platform.

Think of it as a Carousell but for home services. Homeowners can browse and book vendors. Vendors can list their services and manage requests. Everyone gets what they need without having to hunt across ten different platforms.

Building the two-sided marketplace logic, making sure both user types had the right views and the right permissions.

**Tech stack:** React.js, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, Mongoose

[GitHub](https://github.com/Serenula/Skillful-Hands)

## Flights Search Engine

A flight search engine that hunts down the best prices from various airlines. Save and snag the cheapest deal for your next trip.

I travel enough to find flight searching genuinely annoying. Jumping between tabs, losing the price you found ten minutes ago, and then watching it go up by the time you are ready to book.

This was my attempt at fixing that frustration. Pull in prices from multiple sources, surface the best deals, and let users save what they find before it disappears.

**Tech stack:** React.js, TanStack, REST APIs

[GitHub](https://github.com/xxiuminn/flights-search-engine)
